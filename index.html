Software and Systems Work

ML Surrogates for Interconnect Parasitics

A 2D finite-volume capacitance field solver in C++17 generates 4,292 labeled geometries, then a PyTorch MLP predicts line capacitance within 0.14% of the solver on held-out tests — replacing a ~1.8 s solve with a 0.5 µs prediction — and holds under 1% just outside the training range, where an XGBoost baseline drifts past 20%.

Four Small LLMs on One 8GB Card

Benchmarked Gemma 4 E4B and three Qwen models on schema-constrained extraction over 78 hand-labeled documents. Gemma led at 95% precision, beating a model twice its size.
